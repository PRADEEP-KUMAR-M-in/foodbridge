How to read this

Each simulated week uses the same organisations and listings for every row, so rows are fairly compared. Numbers are the median over the simulated weeks, with the 5th to 95th percentile range in brackets. "Largest share" is the portion of all delivered food that went to a single organisation. The FOODBRIDGE rows use the app's real scoring code. Not modelled: donor effort, vehicles and drivers, app adoption, double-dispatch in today's process (which would favour FOODBRIDGE), and the faster listing from Speak-to-List.
